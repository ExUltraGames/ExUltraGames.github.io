The Game & How to Play

RULES is rules... and what the Cats say goes...

GAME PLAY is simple, you have 2 guesses per cat in the image (i.e. 3 cats = 6 guesses), find them all before you run out and you Win, if not the Cats Win.

See the in-app game tutorial (will play first time and is accessible in the top right corner of the game screen) or visit our Youtube channel (in development) for more.

PLAY DAILY - CamoCats is a daily game of one image per day, you can keep playing the game all day, if you lose simply "try again". Progress is saved if you leave, come back anytime during the day to "Continue".

LAST GAME - the solution to each game you play is available the next day, just click "last game".

Achievements & Leader Boards
